## Direction contract

THESIS: a living skin that changes pattern with context, the way Peter changes context (web, mobile, ERP, Web3, business). It refuses the dark dev portfolio with a neon accent and the split hero (text left, photo right, cards below).

OWN-WORLD: pigment against mantle. A chroma-ink field (#7A3A2E) drenches the first viewport. Reading sections use a pigmented light mantle tinted toward the ink hue, never neutral cream. Ridge dark (#2B1E1A) for ink and the close. A single iridophore teal flash, used only for "available" and the primary action. Chromatophore dots as the only texture, flat fields that never blend: no gradient, no glass, no blur. One variable sans whose weight tracks pigment expansion. Wide-tracked caps for short labels only.
